# SME PROBLEM MAP — MYANMAR

## PURPOSE

This file contains research hypotheses and possible pain points
for Myanmar SMEs and entrepreneurs.

These are NOT automatically proven facts.

During the hackathon, use these as starting points to identify
a strong problem that can be solved within the 4-hour build window.

Do not assume every hypothesis is universally true.

---

# 1. MICRO PAIN POINTS

## 1.1 Unrecorded Micro-Expenses

### Hypothesis

Small business expenses such as:

- water
- electricity
- transportation
- refreshments
- delivery
- miscellaneous purchases

may be inconsistently recorded.

### Possible Impact

Small recurring expenses can become difficult to understand
when accumulated over time.

### Possible AI Opportunity

AI could:

- classify messy expense descriptions
- extract amount/category/date
- summarize spending patterns
- identify unusual recurring expenses
- highlight potentially avoidable spending

### MVP Potential

HIGH

### 4-Hour Difficulty

LOW

### Main Risk

Need realistic expense data and avoid unsupported assumptions
about actual savings.

# 2. STAFF HANDOVER GAP

## Hypothesis

When employees change roles or leave,
business owners may struggle to reconstruct:

- cash records
- inventory status
- pending tasks
- customer follow-ups
- unresolved issues

when information is distributed across notebooks,
messages, or verbal communication.

### Possible AI Opportunity

AI could transform unstructured handover notes into:

- inventory checklist
- cash checklist
- unresolved issues
- priority actions
- next-owner briefing

### MVP Potential

HIGH

### Difficulty

LOW–MEDIUM

### Demo Strength

HIGH

# 3. INFORMAL CREDIT TRACKING

## Hypothesis

Some small businesses may track customer credit informally
through notebooks, messages, or memory.

### Possible AI Opportunity

AI can convert messy natural-language records into:

- customer
- amount
- date
- due status
- reminder priority
- collection action

### Example Input

"Ko Min took 3 boxes last Friday.
He will pay next week."

### Example Output

Customer:
Ko Min

Amount:
[if known]

Status:
Pending

Next Action:
Follow up next week

### MVP Potential

HIGH

### Difficulty

LOW

### Key Product Question

Can the system turn unstructured records into
useful financial actions?

# 5. INVISIBLE COST INFLATION

## Hypothesis

Changes in supplier or input costs may not always be
reflected quickly in selling prices.

### Possible AI Opportunity

AI could compare:

Previous Cost
vs
Current Cost
vs
Selling Price

and identify:

- margin compression
- possible underpricing
- products requiring review

### MVP Potential

VERY HIGH

### Difficulty

LOW

### Demo Strength

HIGH

### Important Constraint

Do not present financial conclusions as guaranteed outcomes.

Show them as indicators or recommendations based on
the user's supplied data.

# 9. BANK / FINANCIAL READINESS

## Hypothesis

Some businesses may not maintain consistent,
structured business records.

This may make it harder to present their business
information clearly when seeking external financing
or formal financial services.

### Possible AI Opportunity

AI could transform business records into:

- income summary
- expense summary
- cashflow summary
- receivable summary
- inventory summary

and provide a:

"Financial Readiness Report"

### IMPORTANT

Do NOT claim:

"Bank will approve your loan."

Instead:

"Helps organize business information for financial review."

### MVP Potential

HIGH

### Risk

Do not make unsupported claims about bank eligibility,
loan approval, or creditworthiness.

# INITIAL PRIORITY

Prefer problem areas that have:

1. clear user pain
2. measurable business consequence
3. meaningful AI opportunity
4. simple input
5. clear output
6. strong visual demo
7. realistic 4-hour implementation

---

# DO NOT ASSUME

Do not automatically assume:

- all Myanmar SMEs have the same problems
- all SMEs use informal accounting
- all businesses lack bank access
- all businesses need the same solution
- AI is better than deterministic logic
- a recommendation guarantees financial results

Treat these as hypotheses to validate and frame carefully.
